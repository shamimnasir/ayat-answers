Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Introduction

Al Quran ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application Al Quran - القرآن الكريم ("the App").

Information We Collect

Al Quran is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account required: You can use the app without creating an account or providing any personal information.
  • No personal data collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identifiers.
  • No tracking: We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or any form of user tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data: We do not collect personal data, so we have none to sell or share. The services described under Search and Quran Recitation below are used only to provide those features.

Local Storage

The App stores your preferences (such as bookmarks and theme settings) locally on your device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Can be cleared by clearing the app's data

Search

Search runs on the Quran text stored in the App. When your device is online, the words you search for are also sent to the public Al Quran Cloud API (api.alquran.cloud) to find more matching verses. They are not linked to your identity by us.

Quran Recitation

When you listen to a recitation, the audio is streamed from the Quran.com audio service (verses.quran.com). As with any web request, that service receives your device's IP address. Surahs you download are stored only on your device and can be removed at any time.

Ratings

The App may show Google Play's standard review prompt. Any rating you choose to leave is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Our App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
